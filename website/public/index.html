Get to Know Me

Ashtanga Yoga Practitioner

I am from Tumkur, Karnataka, India.

Yoga was first introduced to me during my childhood by my uncle, who served as my mentor for five years. He is my initial yoga guru, and the knowledge imparted during those formative years has profoundly influenced my life. Since 2015, I have been dedicated to practicing Vinyasa Yoga, continuously deepening my understanding and appreciation of this discipline.

Practice should never end in yoga—whether for a teacher or a student, consistent practice is the key to success.
